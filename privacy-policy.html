Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Welcome to Stream-Agri ("we," "our," or "us"). Stream-Agri is a mobile application designed for agricultural management, enabling field officers to manage grower information, track agricultural inputs, conduct monitoring and evaluation surveys, and facilitate data synchronization with servers.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

By using Stream-Agri, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the application.

2. Information We Collect

We collect various types of information to provide and improve our services:

2.1 Personal Information

We collect the following personal information:

  • User Account Information: Phone number, password, employee details (name, employee ID)
  • Grower/Farmer Information: Full name, national ID number, mobile phone number, physical address
  • Farm Details: Farm size, crop types, farming practices, production data

2.2 Location Information

We collect location data when you use the app, including:

  • GPS Coordinates: Precise location of farms, grower visits, and field activities
  • Location Access: We use both coarse and fine location permissions to accurately record farm locations and field officer activities

2.3 Photos and Media

The app captures and stores:

  • Photographs: Grower portraits, national ID photos, farm location images, and input documentation photos
  • Signatures: Digital signatures for input receipt and distribution records

2.4 Agricultural Data

  • Input Records: Agricultural inputs issued, received, and returned by growers
  • Monitoring Data: Survey responses, evaluation reports, field observations
  • Production Information: Crop yields, farming activities, and production records

2.5 Device Information

  • Device Identifiers: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers
  • Network Information: Internet connectivity status, network type (WiFi/mobile data)

2.6 Usage Data

  • App Activity: Features used, sync history, timestamps of actions
  • Performance Data: Crash reports, error logs (if any)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

3.1 Core Application Functions

  • To register and authenticate field officers and users
  • To manage grower profiles and agricultural production records
  • To track and document agricultural input distribution and returns
  • To conduct monitoring and evaluation activities
  • To generate reports and export data for administrative purposes

3.2 Data Synchronization

  • To sync data between mobile devices and server databases
  • To enable offline functionality and automatic data backup
  • To ensure data consistency across multiple users and devices

3.3 Service Improvement

  • To analyze usage patterns and improve app functionality
  • To identify and fix technical issues
  • To enhance user experience and optimize performance

3.4 Communication

  • To send important notifications about app updates or changes
  • To provide technical support and respond to user inquiries
  • To communicate with growers regarding agricultural programs

4. Data Storage and Security

4.1 Local Storage

  • Data is stored locally on your device using encrypted SQLite databases
  • Sensitive credentials are stored using secure storage mechanisms
  • Photos and media are stored in protected application directories

4.2 Server Storage

  • Data is synchronized with secure servers for backup and multi-device access
  • Server connections use HTTPS encryption for data transmission
  • Data is stored on servers configured by your organization administrator

4.3 Security Measures

  • Password-protected user authentication
  • Encrypted data transmission between app and servers
  • Access controls limiting data access to authorized personnel only
  • Regular security updates and patches

Note: While we implement industry-standard security measures, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your data.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

5.1 Within Your Organization

  • Data is shared with authorized field officers, supervisors, and administrators within your agricultural organization
  • Grower information is accessible to personnel involved in agricultural programs

5.2 Service Providers

  • Cloud Infrastructure: We use third-party cloud services (PowerSync, server hosting) to store and sync data
  • Authentication Services: We use authentication services to verify user credentials

5.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law or in response to:

  • Court orders, subpoenas, or legal processes
  • Requests from government authorities
  • Protection of our legal rights and safety
  • Prevention of fraud or security threats

5.4 Business Transfers

In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, user data may be transferred to the acquiring entity, subject to the same privacy protections.

6. Data Retention

  • Active Data: User and grower information is retained as long as accounts are active
  • Historical Records: Agricultural records may be retained for multiple seasons for historical analysis
  • Deletion Requests: Users may request deletion of their data by contacting their administrator
  • Automatic Deletion: Inactive accounts may be deleted after a period determined by your organization

7. Your Rights and Choices

7.1 Access and Correction

  • You have the right to access your personal information stored in the app
  • You can update or correct grower information and other data through the app interface
  • Contact your administrator to request access to data not visible in the app

7.2 Data Export

  • The app provides a database export function to backup your data
  • You can export data for offline storage or transfer purposes

7.3 Permission Controls

  • Camera: You can revoke camera permission in device settings, but photo features will be disabled
  • Location: You can disable location access, but GPS-based features will not function
  • Storage: The app requires storage access to function properly

7.4 Account Deletion

  • To delete your account and associated data, contact your organization administrator
  • Upon deletion, your personal information will be removed from active systems
  • Some data may be retained for legal or administrative purposes as required

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately, and we will take steps to remove such information.
