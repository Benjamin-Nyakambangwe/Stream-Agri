Transform your agricultural field operations with Stream-Agri - the comprehensive mobile solution designed for field officers, agricultural extension workers, and agribusiness managers. Work seamlessly online or offline, with automatic data synchronization when connectivity returns.

📱 DESIGNED FOR AGRICULTURAL PROFESSIONALS
Stream-Agri empowers field officers to efficiently manage grower relationships, track agricultural inputs distribution, conduct monitoring surveys, and maintain accurate production records - all from a single mobile application.

🌾 KEY FEATURES

✓ WORKS OFFLINE
- Full functionality without internet connection
- Automatic data sync when online
- Never lose data in remote farming areas
- Real-time network status indicator

✓ GROWER MANAGEMENT
Comprehensive farmer database at your fingertips:

- Record farm details: size, location, crop types, and practices

- Search and filter growers instantly
- Track grower production history across seasons
- Update grower information in real-time

- Automatic GPS tagging of distribution locations
- Generate input distribution reports
- Monitor input usage and accountability

📊 HOW IT WORKS

1. ADMIN SETUP
Administrators configure server connections and create field officer accounts through a secure configuration screen.

2. FIELD OFFICER LOGIN
Field officers log in with their phone number and password. First-time login downloads necessary data; subsequent logins work offline.

3. MANAGE GROWERS
Register new farmers, capture photos and documents, record farm details, and maintain comprehensive grower profiles.

4. TRACK INPUTS
Document every input transaction - issued, received, or returned - with photos, signatures, and GPS coordinates.

5. CONDUCT SURVEYS
Perform field monitoring, complete evaluation surveys, and record observations with photographic evidence.

6. SYNC DATA
When online, all collected data automatically syncs to your organization's server, ensuring data consistency across all devices.

🌍 OFFLINE CAPABILITY
Stream-Agri understands that agricultural work often happens in areas with poor connectivity. That's why we built the app to work completely offline after initial setup. All features remain fully functional without internet, and your data syncs automatically when you're back online.

🔒 DATA PRIVACY & SECURITY
We take data protection seriously. All information is encrypted during transmission and storage. Your grower data, photos, and records are secure and accessible only to authorized personnel in your organization.
